Done when: frame() never decodes a PNG on the calling thread; the next frame is decoded in advance on a background thread, or all frames are decoded once at startup, and the render path only uses a ready bitmap. Checked on a Quest 2 by logging the longest time spent in frame() during the startup animation, before and after the change.
