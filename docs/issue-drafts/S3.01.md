title: "S3.01: Stop the Bluetooth radio request from blocking the D-Bus loop"
labels: size:hard, kind:state, platform:linux
---
What: I want the Bluetooth bridge to wait for an Android reply without blocking its D-Bus loop, so other clients still get replies while a pair, connect, forget or discovery request is pending. Today `command()` spins for up to 5 seconds on the one thread that also serves NetworkManager, UPower and BlueZ traffic.
Where: packages/quest-runtime/linux/bluetooth-bridge.cpp:152 (the wait loop in `command`), with its callers in the BlueZ method handlers and the bus loop (code map: docs/code-map.md#quest-linux-bridges)
Size: Hard (about 200 changed lines; C++; D-Bus event loop; platform linux; needs a headset to check; the Android reply timing is an unknown)
Serves: M7
Done when: `command()` returns as soon as it has published its request. The reply is completed from the loop through the existing pending list, which pair and connect already use, and the timeout moves onto the pending entry. While a request is pending, a GetAll from another client gets a reply within one loop period (100 ms). Checked on a Quest 2 with dbus-send during a pairing request.
