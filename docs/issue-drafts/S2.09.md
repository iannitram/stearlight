title: "S2.09: Copy decoded frames into the capture buffer outside the device lock"
labels: size:medium, kind:state, platform:linux
---
What: I want the emulated video decoder to copy each decoded frame without holding the device mutex. Today the copy of an approximately 1.4 MB NV12 frame and the two DMA-BUF sync calls run under the lock, so the app's QBUF, DQBUF and ioctl calls wait for them.
Where: packages/quest-runtime/linux/video-bridge.cpp:125 (lock scope opened in the decode loop), :155-163 (DMA-BUF sync and frame copy under that lock) (code map: docs/code-map.md#quest-linux-bridges)
Size: Medium (about 40 changed lines; C++; platform linux; needs a Quest 2 to check)
Serves: M4
Done when: the device mutex is not held during the memcpy or the DMA_BUF_IOCTL_SYNC calls. The capture buffer chosen for the copy is marked busy under the lock, so a QBUF, DQBUF or STREAMOFF cannot change it during the copy. Checked on a Quest 2 with QUEST_VIDEO_TRACE on: the app's time waiting in DQBUF per frame drops, and decoded frames still come out in order.
