title: "S2.11: Stop the emulated poll() from waking every millisecond while idle"
labels: size:medium, kind:state, platform:linux
---
What: I want the emulated poll() to sleep until a device signals, instead of sleeping 1 ms per pass. Each pass allocates a new pollfd vector, locks every device mutex and sleeps 1 ms, so a blocking poll on a decoder fd wakes about a thousand times a second while nothing is ready.
Where: packages/quest-runtime/linux/video-bridge.cpp:286-289 (the pass loop in the emulated `poll`) (code map: docs/code-map.md#quest-linux-bridges)
Size: Medium (about 50 changed lines; C++; platform linux)
Serves: M4
Done when: while no decoder fd is ready, an emulated poll() with a timeout wakes only when a device signals or the timeout expires. A frame that becomes ready returns from poll() without the 1 ms delay. Checked on a Quest 2 with the player idle: the poll thread shows near-zero wakeups under pidstat, and a ready frame returns from poll() on the next signal.
