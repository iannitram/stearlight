title: "S2.07: Read the socket batching switch once per process"
labels: size:medium, kind:state, platform:linux
---
What: I want the socket batching hook to read QUEST_STREAM_SOCKET_BATCH once, not on every recv() and recvfrom() call. When the streaming launcher sets the variable, each receive on the player pays a getenv and at least one getsockopt before the real call; the gain from removing the getenv alone is small.
Where: packages/quest-runtime/linux/socket-report.c:147-150 (`stream_datagram`, called from `recv` and `recvfrom`) (code map: docs/code-map.md#quest-linux-bridges)
Size: Medium (about 20 changed lines; C; platform linux)
Serves: M4
Done when: `stream_datagram` reads the variable on its first call and keeps the result for the life of the process. Receive results are the same with the variable set and unset. Checked on a Quest 2: the streaming player runs with no change in frame delivery, and a host test that sets the variable and receives on a UDP socket and a pipe gets identical data both ways.
